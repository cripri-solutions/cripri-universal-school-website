About the platform

A complete digital presence for every institution.

CRIPRI brings institution websites, public information and content management together. Each institution has its own identity, website address, approved content and authorised managers.

Institutions can apply through the registration process. Applications are reviewed before activation, and institution managers use a secure sign-in to update their own website.
