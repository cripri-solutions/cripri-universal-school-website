About the platform

More than a website template.

Our goal is to provide schools with a consistent starting point for their online presence, while allowing each school to present its own identity, information and community.

The platform is being built in stages. The public website design is available as a preview; secure registration, administrator accounts and live content management will be introduced only after the backend and security controls are ready.

Platform capabilities

Designed around the needs of schools.

The platform roadmap brings the public school website and its administration together.

01

Professional school website

Present the school profile, learning programmes, admissions information, news and contact details.

02

School identity

Give each school its own name, logo, colours, photographs and approved public content.

03

Administrator workspace

A planned secure portal for authorised school administrators to manage website information.

04

Central school onboarding

Schools apply to join the platform and receive a decision from CRIPRI before activation.

05

School-by-school separation

The planned backend will restrict each school to its own accounts and permitted information.

06

Room to grow

A foundation that can be extended carefully as new school website features are designed and tested.

Joining CRIPRI

A reviewed onboarding process.

CRIPRI intends to verify every school before granting access. Submitting an application will not automatically activate a school.

1

Apply

The school provides its official details and an authorised contact person.

2

Verification

CRIPRI reviews the school information and verifies the representative.

3

Decision

CRIPRI records an approval or rejection. Applications remain pending until reviewed.

4

Setup

Approved schools proceed to a controlled website and administrator setup process.
