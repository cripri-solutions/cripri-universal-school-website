The CRIPRI platform

More than a static website.

CRIPRI connects public institution websites with managed content, school-specific identities and a reviewed onboarding process. Each active institution receives its own public website address and publishes information through authorised access.

Platform capabilities

Built for day-to-day institutional communication.

01

Public institution website

Present your profile, facilities, programmes, contacts, announcements, events, vacancies and gallery.

02

Managed content

Authorised administrators create, review, publish, withdraw and recover website content.

03

Institution identity

Use your approved institution name, slug, logo, banner, motto and public contact details.

04

Reviewed onboarding

Applications are reviewed by CRIPRI. Submission alone does not activate an institution.

05

Separated access

School administrators and content editors receive role-based access to their own institution.

06

Subscription and domains

Website access is governed by the institution's subscription and configured website address.
