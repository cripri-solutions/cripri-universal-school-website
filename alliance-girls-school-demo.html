About the school

A tradition of learning and service.

Alliance Girls High School was founded in 1948 by the Alliance of Protestant Missions in Kenya. Its story forms part of the development of girls' education in the country.

The school brings together learners from different regions and backgrounds, with an emphasis on academic achievement, character, unity and contribution to society.
