5. Public content control

Demonstration controls for public photographs and school communications. Pull Down removes an item from the public preview; Restore returns it. Permanent deletion asks for confirmation.

Prototype limitation: This demonstration stores sample records and its activity log in this browser only. It is not connected to administrator sign-in, a shared database or the live public website. Do not use it to manage real school content.
